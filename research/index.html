✧ Field guide

Work from the record.

  1. Make the question small. Start with one name, one event, and the place or year you already know.
  2. Find the source behind the index. Search both Jesionowski and Jesionowska. If the index points to a marriage act, check the scan for parents, witnesses, and places.
  3. Keep disagreements visible. Write down exactly what each source says. Compare dates, relationships, places, and who supplied the information before joining two records.

A worked example

Śniadowo, 1824

Finding. The marriage excerpt names Brygida’s parents. An 1804 baptism index gives those same parents for Brygida and her twin Balbina, under the spelling Jasionowska.

Citation. Śniadowo parish marriage register, 1824, entry 5, as excerpted in the Łomża surname catalogue, PDF pages 53–54.

Next check. Compare the indexed spellings and request both the full 1824 marriage act and any attachments. The Śniadowo records point to the Łomża Diocesan Archive.
