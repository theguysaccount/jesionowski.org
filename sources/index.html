✦ Six records · 1477–1926

The first entries

1477 · Lwów / Lviv

Iwasko in the Lwów register ↗

Printed court transcription

A court entry contains an early written form of the Jesionowski surname.

1593 · Kraków

Łukasz and his father Jan ↗

University register index

A university enrollment index gives Łukasz Jesionowski’s recorded name, his father’s given name, and the diocese he came from.

1820 · Śniadowo / Jakać Stara

Katarzyna’s marriage record ↗

Marriage excerpts and parish indexes

The marriage index names Stanisław Swierdzewski. A later Puchały death entry identifies Katarzyna by her parents and widower.

1824 · Śniadowo / Zalesie Wypychy

Brygida’s marriage record ↗

Marriage excerpts and parish indexes

Brygida married Kacper Cichosz in 1824. An earlier baptism index names her twin, Balbina.

1921 · Poznań / Mogilno

Alfred’s enrollment card ↗

Reproduced enrollment card

Alfred Jesionowski’s university form records his birthplace and names his parents.

1926 · Olszaki

Stanisław’s school signature ↗

Handwritten signature and index

A school sheet in the Polish Declarations carries the handwritten name Stanisław Jesionowski.
