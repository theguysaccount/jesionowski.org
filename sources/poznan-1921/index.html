October 7, 1921 · Poznań / Mogilno

Alfred’s enrollment card.

Alfred Jesionowski’s university form records his birthplace and names his parents.

✦ The evidence

Read the record

Wojciech i Maria Jesionowscy

“Wojciech and Maria Jesionowski” is the entry in the parents’ field. The card gives Mogilno as Alfred’s birthplace and January 27, 1902, as his date of birth.

What it supports

The card connects Alfred with Wojciech and Maria. The biography gives Maria’s birth surname as Mensch and describes the family spelling change from Jeschinowski to Jesionowski in 1921.

What’s still open

The card gives the parents’ names as entered in 1921. A birth or baptism record from 1902 could show the spelling used at the time and provide a separate check on the relationship.
