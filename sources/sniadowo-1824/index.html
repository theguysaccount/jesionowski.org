1824 · Śniadowo / Zalesie Wypychy

Brygida’s marriage record.

Brygida Jesionowska married Kacper Cichosz in Śniadowo. An earlier parish index records her and her twin Balbina in Jakać Młoda.

✦ The evidence

Read the record

Pawła Jesionowskiego (…) i Kunegundy z Cendrowskich

The names are Paweł Jesionowski and Kunegunda, born into the Cendrowski family. The excerpt describes Brygida as their daughter and places the parents in Zalesie Wypychy.

What the records support

The marriage index pairs Brygida, indexed as Jasionowska, with Kacper Cichosz. The catalogue’s Cichosz excerpt names his parents, Antoni Cichosz and Anna née Woźniak, and cites a certificate from Szczepankowo. Its Łukasiewicz excerpt names witness Błażej Łukasiewicz.

Follow the names

1804 · Śniadowo baptism index, entry 3286. The index lists Brygida and Balbina Jasionowska as twins in Jakać Młoda, daughters of Paweł and Kunegunda Cędrowska. The matching parents identify Brygida across the two entries. The marriage excerpt places her with her parents in Zalesie Wypychy twenty years later. Read the baptism index.

1796–1802 · Earlier clues. A 1796 marriage index pairs Kunegunda Cedrowska with Kacper Andrzejewski in Jakać Stara. The 1800 burial index names them as parents of a two-year-old Stanisław Andrzejewski in Jakać Młoda. A 1799 marriage index for Jakać Młoda names the widow Kunegunda Andrzejewska and Wawrzyniec Jasionowski. The first name Wawrzyniec needs to be checked against Paweł in the later records.

Possible older sister. An 1801 baptism index for Jakać Młoda names Marianna Jasionowska as the daughter of Paweł and Kunegunda Andrzejewska. An 1802 burial index gives the same village and parents’ given names, with the child indexed as Marianna Jasińska and the mother as Cendrowska. The entries are promising leads with differing surname forms.

What’s still open

The originals may explain how the Andrzejewska and Cendrowska names relate and whether the 1799 groom and Paweł were the same person. Brygida’s grandparents and any link to Katarzyna’s household remain unconfirmed.
