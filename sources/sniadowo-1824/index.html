1824 · Śniadowo / Zalesie Wypychy

Brygida’s marriage record.

A marriage excerpt names Brygida Jesionowska’s parents and places the household in Zalesie Wypychy.

✦ The evidence

Read the record

Pawła Jesionowskiego (…) i Kunegundy z Cendrowskich

The names are Paweł Jesionowski and Kunegunda, born into the Cendrowski family. The excerpt describes Brygida as their daughter and places the parents in Zalesie Wypychy.

What it supports

The passage records Brygida as the daughter of Paweł and Kunegunda. It gives Kunegunda’s birth family and the village where the parents lived.

What’s still open

The full act can supply further details about the marriage and witnesses. The relationship between this household and the 1820 Jakać Stara household remains open.
