1926 · Olszaki

Stanisław’s school signature.

A school sheet in the Polish Declarations carries the handwritten name Stanisław Jesionowski.

✦ The evidence

Read the record

Stanisław Jesionowski

KARTA reads this name on the elementary school sheet for Olszaki. The sheet belongs to the declarations presented to President Calvin Coolidge for the U.S. sesquicentennial.

What it supports

This spelling appears on a dated school sheet associated with Olszaki. The scan lets readers compare the handwriting with the index.

What’s still open

The signature gives a name and a school context. His age, household, and relationship to any other Stanisław Jesionowski remain open.

✧ Archive reference

Find it again

Source type
Handwritten signature and index
Record reference
Polish Declarations of Admiration and Friendship for the United States, volume 59, page 221. The KARTA index lists it as signature 6.
Where we read it
The Library of Congress preserves the declaration sheet. The Polska1926 project, run by the KARTA Center, indexes the signature and the school.
