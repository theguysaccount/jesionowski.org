1820 · Śniadowo / Jakać Stara

Katarzyna’s marriage record.

A Śniadowo marriage excerpt names Katarzyna Jesionowska’s parents and points to a second record in Zambrów.

✦ The evidence

Read the record

niegdy uczciwego Andrzeja Jesionowskiego (…) i Ursuli z domu niewiadomey

The excerpt refers to the late Andrzej Jesionowski and Urszula, whose birth surname was unknown. It identifies Katarzyna as their daughter and refers to a certificate from the Zambrów church registers.

What it supports

The excerpt ties Katarzyna to Andrzej and Urszula, gives Jakać Stara as her residence, and supplies a reference to the Zambrów church books.

What’s still open

Urszula’s birth surname is unknown in the excerpt. The relationship between this Katarzyna and the name recorded in Łomża in 1790 remains open.
