1820 · Śniadowo / Jakać Stara

Katarzyna’s marriage record.

Katarzyna Jesionowska married Stanisław Swierdzewski in Śniadowo. The 1820 excerpt names her parents and cites a Zambrów certificate.

✦ The evidence

Read the record

niegdy uczciwego Andrzeja Jesionowskiego (…) i Ursuli z domu niewiadomey

The excerpt refers to the late Andrzej Jesionowski and Urszula, whose birth surname was unknown. It identifies Katarzyna as their daughter and refers to a certificate from the Zambrów church registers.

What the records support

The 1820 marriage index identifies the groom as Stanisław Swierdzewski and lists Franciszka Swierdzewska in his parents’ field. The excerpt names the bride’s late father, Andrzej, and places her mother Urszula in Jakać Stara.

Follow the names

1833 · Puchały, death entry 95. The index records Katarzyna Świerdzewska, also indexed as Jasionowska, who died in Wyrzyki on August 11. Her parents are Andrzej and Urszula, and her widower is Stanisław. These matches tie the entry to the 1820 bride. The index gives her age as 32. The marriage excerpt describes her as of age, so her birth year remains unconfirmed. Read the Puchały death index.

1790 · Łomża, baptism reference 1098. The indexed child Catharina Brigitta Jesionowska has parents Casimirus and Agnes (Kazimierz and Agnieszka). Those names distinguish her from the Śniadowo bride. Read the Łomża baptism index.

Possible siblings. Three indexes also name Andrzej and Urszula as parents of a Jesionowski or Jasionowski child. They are Franciszka, Zambrów death entry 33 of 1813, Gniewoty, Bartłomiej, Zambrów marriage index entry 189 of 1820, Pstrągi, and Małgorzata, Puchały death entry 28 of 1830, Zbrzeźnica. The full acts may connect these households through a shared village, witness, or additional relative.

What’s still open

Urszula’s birth surname and the grandparents remain unknown. The 1810 Zambrów death index for an Andrzej Jesionowski gives Pstrągi, the same place as Bartłomiej’s 1820 marriage index. A spouse or other identifying detail in the full death act is needed to establish whether he was Katarzyna’s father.
