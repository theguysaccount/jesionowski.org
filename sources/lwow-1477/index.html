January 3, 1477 · Lwów / Lviv

Iwasko in the Lwów register.

A court entry contains an early written form of the Jesionowski surname.

✦ The evidence

Read the record

Iwasko Iesyonowsky

The printed entry is headed Pena Regalis, meaning “royal penalty.” The name is reproduced here as it appears in the printed edition.

What it supports

The spelling Iesyonowsky appears in a dated Lwów court entry. The modern Institute of Polish Language surname entry indexes it under Jesionowski.

What’s still open

Iwasko’s family relationships, home village, and any connection to a later Jesionowski branch are open questions.
