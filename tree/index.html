Jack supplied the relationships in his immediate family. Mary Lou’s obituary names her parents, her husband Ben, and their sons Lee and Stan. An Air Force Reserve newsletter from 2005 also identifies Trent as Stan’s son.

Mary Lou’s obituary and photo · Air Force Reserve newsletter (PDF)

In the 1950 Hamilton County marriage record, Ben gave his birthplace as Glassport, Pennsylvania, and named his parents as Paul Jesionowski and Anna Jozwiak. Mary Louise Hillen named her parents as Anthony Hillen and Emma Hertmann. Frank’s obituary records his 1927 birth in Glassport, names Paul and Anna as his parents, and identifies Ben as his brother. Mary Lou’s obituary describes Ben’s parents as Polish American.

Ben and Mary Lou’s marriage record, volume 561, page 39 · Frank Jesionowski’s obituary · Ben’s veterans’ burial entry

Pennsylvania’s death indexes point to two possible certificates for Ben’s parents. Paul Jesionowski’s entry is dated September 5, 1951, in Collier Township (certificate 74869). Anna Jesionowski’s entry is dated September 4, 1966, in McKeesport (certificate 86222). The full certificates may identify their birthplaces or parents and help trace the family back to Poland.

Paul’s death index (PDF) · Anna’s death index (PDF) · Pennsylvania State Archives certificate copies
